# The first standing HPC allocation (milestone steps for #179), 14 Nov 2026

One standing allocation ran the HPC bundle as a federation of one zone and three providers, with the agent's cell on a
fifth node, from 23:04Z to 23:16Z. Nodes are named by role: **Z** the zone, **P1** to **P3** the providers, **S** the
agent's sandbox, which hosts no Onedata service. "Unknown" and "not checked" mean not measured. No host, address,
account, grant, partition, path, job id, space id or file id is recorded here, and no token. The driver's steps are now
in `deploy/hpc-federation/run/`, rewritten with an evidence rule (every step writes raw evidence to files and the summary
is derived from them); this file records what the run measured, as audited afterwards from its saved raw evidence.

**Corrected outcome (audit, 14 Nov, from the saved raw evidence and the driver's scripts):**

- **The gate-log token scan: UNVERIFIED (a false pass).** The driver's summary printed `"token_in_log": False` as a
  literal. The gate's log was read for line counts and event kinds only, never scanned for the token, and the log was
  removed with the sandbox's private directory, so "no token in the gate's log" is unverified for this run.
- **Stands, raw evidence saved:** the write through the gate refused (403); direct requests without a token refused (401,
  401); every connect result per host and port (Couchbase 8091 and 11210 and the default epmd 4369 refused everywhere,
  the internet's port 443 open); the round trip (written and read SHA-256 saved and equal on P2 and P3, statuses 200);
  the teardown's per-node counts (0 everywhere, 0 steps left besides the batch step).
- **Stands, computed but the input not saved:** the content read through the gate (`matches` was computed in the cell
  from SHA-256 of the body against the expected one; only the status 200, the byte count and the boolean were saved);
  the Couchbase guard and the default checks (the bundle's `ok:` lines; their status codes were not saved as data).
- **Inconclusive:** client-token stripping (a 403 that may be the gate policy's own refusal of the route).
- **Not in the saved evidence:** the final "no job of ours remains".
- **No token in any saved log** (0 matches for the token keys).

## Plan

- Allocation: 5 nodes (Z, P1-P3, S), no spare; 8 cores and 20 GB per node (billing about 10 per node; the seventh live
  run's peaks were about 4.0 and 4.8 GB of RSS plus the 1.9 and 2.5 GB rootfs on tmpfs); 8 h; `--no-requeue`; held by a
  `sleep`. Approved by the user as a standing allocation for #179's milestone tests, released when idle for about 2 h.
- Every step an `srun --jobid --overlap -w <node>`, detached on the login node (`setsid nohup`), proven first with a
  2-minute sleep step that had to survive the ssh session's end.
- Order: upload, rootfs builds (steps on P2 and P3), the zone, three providers (tokens minted in a zone step and piped
  into a provider step; delivery steps given 1 GB, as the seventh run found), the round trips, the agent's cell on S,
  egress detection (if the shim logs), teardown and verification, release.
- The bundle was PR 1's, at `4af4728a` (the hashed shipped-value check). It differs from the seventh run's in the guard
  (`hpcop-cb-init.py`, baked into the rootfs), the Python helpers, the sbatch exit status, `teardown.sh`, and `lib.sh`,
  `check-defaults.sh`, `patch.sh` and `baked.py`, so both rootfs tarballs were rebuilt from it.

## Findings

1. PR 1's bundle runs live as merged-to-be: the guard re-ask and the exit-status rule, and the hashed check in prep, in
   the file check and in the live check, on one zone and three providers.
2. **Provider-to-provider transfer works across nodes** through the shim's peer rules: P2's first read waited 8.4 s while
   the blocks moved from P1, and all three providers then hold the whole file.
3. **The core's data gate runs unchanged on a batch node** (python3.11, stdlib only, a high port on loopback) and served
   the cell's content read with its own read-only token. Its policy refuses `GET /spaces/<id>` (so a cell has to start
   from a file id or a route the policy lists, such as the lookup by the space's name) and the write.
4. **From the cell, on its own node, only the public ports are reachable** (8443 everywhere, 6665 on providers).
   Couchbase, memcached, onepanel and epmd are on loopback on their nodes, so the default-credential and default-cookie
   attacks have no port to reach. The internet is reachable: detection is milestone 3's job.
5. **Open items:** the gate log's token scan did not run (a literal in the summariser); the listing through the gate and
   the client-token-stripping probe need a route the policy allows (a listing by the space root's id, which a lookup of
   the space's name gives); the mount path (oneclient) was not part of the run; the Couchbase guard's and the default
   checks' status codes were not saved as data; the final queue check was not saved.
